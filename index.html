About Me

Cybersecurity is my foundation, but service, leadership, entrepreneurship, and education shape how I use it.

I’m Deja Ware, but most people call me D. I’m a computer science student, cybersecurity professional, licensed insurance agent, and emerging entrepreneur who genuinely enjoys helping people feel more informed, prepared, and protected.

Cybersecurity is at the center of what I do. I am interested in securing systems, identifying risks early, and creating solutions that protect the people who depend on technology every day. That same mindset carries into my work in insurance, where I help individuals and families understand their options, prepare for the unexpected, and make confident decisions about their future.

My work also reflects my interest in leadership, education, entrepreneurship, and innovation. I believe valuable ideas can come from anywhere when we are willing to look closely, question what already exists, and think creatively about how things could work better.

Although my interests may appear in different forms, they are connected by one purpose: protecting what matters, solving important problems, and using what I learn to create opportunities for others.
