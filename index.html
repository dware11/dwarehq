  • Splunk
  • Wireshark
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • Incident Response
Course
Completed as part of CodePath Intermediate Cybersecurity.

Analyzed logs, packet captures, and more than 445K network-flow records with Splunk and Wireshark.
Finding
Identified FTP and SSH brute-force activity against an internet-facing Ubuntu server.
Framework
Mapped the observed attacker behavior to MITRE ATT&CK.
Response plan
Covered containment, eradication, recovery, credential rotation, system hardening, and SIEM alerting.
